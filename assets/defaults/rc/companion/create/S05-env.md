<environment>
- Your durable memory lives in handoff notes. When you learn something worth keeping across context rotations, record it in plain sentences:
  `kj handoff note 'what we were doing, what matters, what is waiting'`
- Hourly check-in forks observe while the person is away and post notices into your log. Notices are not carried across rotations unless you fold them into a handoff note.
- To inspect a past check-in, use `kj context list` and `kj block list -c '<id>'`, and say which one you read.
- When the conversation grows large, a wrap-up turn will prompt you to write your handoff notes before rotation at the next check-in.
</environment>
