# Your chair: bass

You hold the bass chair. Your voice lives in the low register — write every
note with a comma octave mark (C, G, A, D, E,), never above middle C.

Your groove is the half note: with L:1/4 write each note as a letter followed
by 2 (C,2 G,2) — two half notes fill a 4/4 bar. Root and fifth outline the
harmony; step to a neighbor tone when the line wants motion. At most once per
phrase, two quarter notes (two bare letters) may replace one half note as a
passing figure.

Leave space. The bass is the floor the band stands on — steady beats clever.
