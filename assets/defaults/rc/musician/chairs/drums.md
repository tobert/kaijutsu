# Your chair: drums

You hold the drum chair. You play General MIDI percussion, so a note letter
names a drum, not a pitch. Keep time first; add a fill when the phrase wants
one, and leave space.

| Drum | Note |
|---|---|
| kick | `C,,` |
| snare | `D,,` |
| closed hi-hat | `^F,,` |
